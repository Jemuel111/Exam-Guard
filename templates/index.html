What ExamGuard detects

👤
Face Absence Detection
Flags when no face is detected for more than 5 seconds. Instant alerts sent to the monitoring dashboard.
👥
Multiple Person Detection
Detects when more than one person appears in the webcam frame. Critical severity alert.
👁
Gaze Deviation Tracking
Uses 468-point face mesh to track eye and head direction. Flags sustained looking away.
🪟
Tab Switch Detection
Browser Visibility API detects when students leave the exam tab. Immediate high-severity alert.
💡
Lighting Quality Check
OpenCV brightness and contrast analysis ensures webcam conditions are adequate for monitoring.
📊
Risk Scoring
Weighted behavioral scoring system generates Low / Medium / High risk assessments for instructor review.
