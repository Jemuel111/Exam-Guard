DETECTION CAPABILITIES
What ExamGuard monitors
NO_FACE
Face Absence

Flags when no face is detected for more than 5 consecutive seconds. High severity alert.

MULTIPLE_FACES
Multiple Persons

Detects when more than one person appears in the webcam frame. Critical severity.

LOOK_AWAY
Gaze Deviation

Face mesh landmark analysis tracks sustained looking away from the screen over 3 seconds.

TAB_SWITCH
Tab Switch

Browser Visibility API detects when the student navigates away from the exam tab. Immediate.

POOR_LIGHTING
Lighting Quality

OpenCV brightness and contrast analysis ensures conditions are adequate for face detection.

RISK_SCORE
Risk Assessment

Weighted behavioral scoring: Low / Medium / High. All flags require human instructor review.
